01 / Profile

Curiosity, evidence, precision.

I hold a PhD in Medicinal Chemistry from the University of Mississippi, an MSc in Biotechnology of Bioactive Products, and a degree in Chemical Engineering from the Federal University of Pernambuco. My laboratory-based research in organic and medicinal chemistry culminated in a postdoctoral appointment at the Federal University of São João del-Rei (2015–2016). After leaving academic research, I began learning Python as a pathway toward remote work. My studies subsequently expanded to data science and machine-learning topics; structured AI study is a goal for the next stage.

Research appointments and academic training are distinct from the course-based programming repositories linked below. See my Lattes academic CV for research publications and academic details.
